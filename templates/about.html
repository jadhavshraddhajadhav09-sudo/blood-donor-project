About LifeBlood Registry

A digital web application designed for rapid emergency blood donor lookup, eligibility validation, and emergency request management.

Project Purpose

Bridging the Gap Between Donors & Patients

In medical emergencies, every minute lost in searching for compatible blood donors increases risk. LifeBlood Registry eliminates manual phone calls and delays by organizing voluntary donors by blood group, locality, and strict 90-day donation eligibility.

  • Instant local search filtered by exact or compatible blood types
  • Automated 90-day cooling period validation to protect donor health
  • Phone number masking & privacy protection
  • Real-time matching for active hospital requests

Eligibility Guidelines

90-Day Donation Rule

Male and female donors must allow a minimum of 90 days (3 months) between whole blood donations to ensure red blood cell replenishment.

Age & Health Criteria

Donors must be between 18 and 65 years of age, weigh at least 45 kg, and have a hemoglobin level above 12.5 g/dL.
